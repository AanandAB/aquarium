1. Logging in

1. Open your website and add /admin/login to the end of the address.

For example, if your site is https://happy-aquarium.workers.dev, go to:

https://happy-aquarium.workers.dev/admin/login
2. Enter your email and password, then click Sign in.
Default login (change it after your first login):
Email: admin@happyaquarium.in
Password: admin123

4. Editing / deleting a fish

1. Go to Fish.
2. Find the fish in the list. Click the Edit button on its row.
3. Change anything, then click Save changes.

To hide a fish from the site without deleting it, toggle the Published checkbox (under "Flags") or click the Live/Hidden button in the list.

To permanently remove a fish, click the red Delete button in the list.

Careful: Delete is permanent and cannot be undone.
↑ top

5. Adding & changing images

Every fish and product has an image field. You have two ways to add a picture:

Option A — Paste a link (fastest)

1. In the edit form, find the Hero image / Product image field.
2. Paste a direct image URL (ends in .jpg, .png, .webp).

Example:

https://example.com/images/my-fish.jpg

Option B — Upload from your phone/computer

1. Click the Upload button next to the image field.
2. Choose the photo from your device.
3. It uploads and the field fills in automatically.
Multiple images: the Gallery URLs field lets you add extra photos — one URL per line.

6. Pair vs single pricing

Some fish are sold per pair (two fish), others per single fish.

1. Edit the fish.
2. Find the Rate type dropdown.
3. Choose Pair (price is for two fish) or Single (price is per fish).
Rate typeUse for
PairGuppies, mollies, tetras, most community fish
SingleBettas, flowerhorns, alligator gar, snails, large predators

7. Variants (sizes, wattages, colour types)

If one product or fish comes in different versions (like a light in 10W, 15W and 20W, or an alligator gar in different sizes), link them together as variants.

For accessories / products

1. First create each version as its own product (e.g. "LED Light 10W", "LED Light 15W", "LED Light 20W").
2. Edit one of them (e.g. "LED Light 10W").
3. Find the Variants field — a searchable dropdown.
4. Click it, search for the other versions, and tick them.
5. Save. Now the 10W page shows "Also available in" with clickable cards for the 15W and 20W.

For fish

Same steps — edit a fish, use the Variants dropdown to pick other fish, save. They show as "Also available in" cards.

How it looks to customers: a small grid of clickable cards, each with the variant's image, name and price. Tapping one opens that item's page.

9. Categories

Categories group your fish and products. The site already has these set up:

  • Fish: Livebearers, Tetras, Cichlids, Goldfish & Koi, Catfish & Loaches, Bettas & Gouramis, Sharks & Suckers, Angelfish & Oddballs, Snails & Invertebrates
  • Products: Aquariums, Filters, Air Pumps, Lighting, Plants, Decor, Food, Medicines, Water Conditioners, Gravel, Cleaning Tools
1. Click Categories in the sidebar.
2. To add a new category, use the form — give it a name, pick the type (Fish or Product), and save.
Tip: a new category appears on the fish/products filter buttons automatically once it has items.

11. Homepage sections

The front page is built from sections you can reorder, edit or hide.

1. Click Homepage in the sidebar.
2. You'll see all sections: Hero, Featured Fish, Categories, Offers, Latest Arrivals, Popular Fish, Accessories, Gallery, Testimonials, Articles, Map, FAQ, Newsletter.
3. For each section you can:
  • Edit the title and subtitle
  • Reorder (change the number)
  • Hide it (toggle off)
Example: to remove the map from the homepage, find the "Map" section and hide it.

12. Gallery, testimonials & FAQs

Gallery

Add customer tank photos, store photos, aquascapes. Click Gallery → add a title, paste the image URL, pick a type (showcase, customer tank, etc.).

Testimonials

Add customer reviews with a name, star rating (1–5), review text, and location. Click Testimonials → New testimonial.

FAQs

Add common questions and answers. Click FAQs → add question + answer. These show in the FAQ section of the homepage.

13. Customer enquiries

When someone fills the contact form or taps "Reserve on WhatsApp", the enquiry appears here.

1. Click Enquiries in the sidebar.
2. You'll see each enquiry with the customer's name, contact, and message.
3. Update the status as you deal with it: New → Contacted → Confirmed → Completed (or Cancelled).

14. Store settings

This is where your store's core info lives.

1. Click Settings in the sidebar.
2. Update:
  • Store name — your shop name
  • Phone / WhatsApp — shown on every page's "Call" and "WhatsApp" buttons
  • Email
  • Address, area, city, state, pincode
  • Map coordinates — for the "Visit our store" map
  • Opening hours
  • Social links — Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp
Important: the WhatsApp number must be in international format with the country code and no + sign. For India: 919947770808 (91 + 10-digit number).
↑ top

15. Changing your password

1. Click Settings.
2. Find the Users section.
3. Edit your user and enter a new password. Save.
Good password: at least 12 characters, mix of letters, numbers and symbols. Don't use "admin123".

16. Tips & troubleshooting

Quick tips

  • Save often. Changes are live immediately after you click Save.
  • Add an image to everything. Pages with photos get far more customer attention.
  • Use offer prices to show a strike-through "was ₹X, now ₹Y" effect.
  • Keep stock numbers honest — "Out of stock" is better than disappointing a customer.

Common problems

ProblemFix
I don't see my changeRefresh the page. If still missing, check the "Published" toggle is on.
Image won't uploadTry pasting the image URL instead, or use a smaller image file.
Fish not showing in a categoryEdit the fish and check the Category dropdown is set.
Can't log inCheck the exact URL — it must end with /admin/login.
Variants not showingMake sure you selected them in the Variants dropdown AND saved.
